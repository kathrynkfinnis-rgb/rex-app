How it works

Rex something you loved and it appears in your friends' feed. Save theirs to your own list for later. Ask the people you trust a question — a blast — and collect their answers in one place. When you're planning a weekend somewhere, open the map and everything anyone has recommended nearby is already on it.

Your data

REX doesn't sell your data or run ads. You can download everything we hold about you, or delete your account and all of it, from inside the app: Profile → Settings → Your data & privacy. The Privacy Policy explains the rest in plain terms.
